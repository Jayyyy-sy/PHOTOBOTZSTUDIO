Camera access

The booth needs your camera to see your hands and take your photos.

Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded or recorded.

💡 A friendly tip: our booth uses your own device's camera, so your photos will look as bright as the space around you. If your picture looks a little dark, we kindly suggest moving to a brighter spot, or shining a flashlight or lamp gently toward your face. A little extra light makes every snap shine!

You seem to be in an in-app browser, which can block the camera and downloads. Tap the menu (••• or the share icon) and choose Open in Chrome or Open in Safari.
